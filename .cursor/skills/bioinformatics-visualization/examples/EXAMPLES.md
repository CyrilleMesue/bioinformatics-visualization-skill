# Style examples

These images are synthetic atlas reproductions. Match their clarity: readable type, panel letters clear of titles and legends, visible uncertainty, and space between panels. Do not copy their labels, sample names, or numbers.

Selection uses the family exemplar grade. Every grade 9 and grade 10 is included. A grade 8 is included only when 8 is the highest grade in that plot family. Standalone examples are one panel. Grouped examples are the panels that were graded together.

A higher grade is not always the better target. After drawing, compare the figure with these examples. Choose the grade 9 example over the grade 10 example when the grade 9 layout stays readable for this data and the grade 10 layout is crowded or hides the comparison.

Standalone: 47. Grouped: 74.
