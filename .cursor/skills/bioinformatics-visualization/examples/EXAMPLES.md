# Style examples

These PNG files are synthetic atlas reproductions or synthetic benchmark assemblies. They show layout, panel letters, typography, and uncertainty. Do not copy their labels, sample names, or numbers into a new figure.

Before drawing, open the PNG for the chosen plot family and the PNG for the panel group when the figure has more than one panel.
